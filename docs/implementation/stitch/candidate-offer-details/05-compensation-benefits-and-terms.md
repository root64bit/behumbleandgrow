# Compensation, Benefits & Employment Terms — Phase A9

## Base Salary & Currency
- Displays base salary formatted in exact AED currency (e.g. `AED 4,500 / month`).
- Omits take-home tax estimations unless deductions are legally defined.

## Structured Allowances & Benefits
- Accommodation: Provided or monthly housing allowance.
- Transport: Provided company transport or monthly allowance.
- Medical Insurance: Gold Tier comprehensive UAE medical insurance.
- Annual Paid Leave: 30 calendar days per year.
- Annual Flight Benefit: Round-trip ticket to home country annually.

## Contractual Terms
- Contract Type: Full-time Unlimited Contract.
- Probation Period: 6 Months (max under UAE Labour Law).
- Working Hours: 40 Hours / Week.
- Overtime Terms: As per UAE Federal Decree Law No. 33 of 2021.
